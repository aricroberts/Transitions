How it works

From uncertain to decided.

No jargon. No sign-up. No spreadsheets. Just honest analysis of where you stand and where you're going.

01
📍
Find your moment
Pick the tool that matches where you are right now — entering, returning, pivoting, building, comparing, or planning your exit.
02
📊
Answer guided questions
No formulas to know. Fill in what you know — salary, timeline, skills, situation — and the tool does the math. Honest estimates beat paralysis.
03
🤖
Get AI-powered coaching
Each tool includes an AI coach that asks one question at a time, helps you think through tradeoffs, and produces output you can actually use.
04
📄
Export what you need
Plans, reports, scripts, and summaries — ready to take into conversations, interviews, meetings, or your own files. Your data, your words.
05
🔒
Walk away, nothing stored
When you close the tab, everything is gone. We never see your data. Nothing is tracked, sold, or kept. It never leaves your browser.
06
🚀
Enter the room ready
Interviews. Negotiations. Family conversations about retirement. You'll have the numbers, the framing, and the clarity to say the right thing.

Who uses Transitions

Built for anyone at a turning point.

Career crossroads don't care about your age, industry, or title. They care whether you're ready.

🎓
The first-generation professional
No one in your family has done this before. You're figuring out offer letters, salary ranges, and workplace norms without a guide. We can be that guide.
👩‍👧
The caregiver returning to work
You stepped out to care for someone you love. The gap on your resume is real — and so is the value you built during it. We help you reframe both.
🔀
The mid-career pivoter
You've spent ten years mastering something, and now you want something else. Your skills transfer. We help you prove it — in language the new industry understands.
📈
The equity-building professional
Stock options, 401k, real estate, side income. You're trying to build wealth while working full time — without a financial advisor you can afford yet.
🎯
The person choosing between offers
Two paths. Different salaries, different locations, different trajectories. You need a framework, not more opinions from people who don't know your situation.
🏁
The person planning their exit
Ready to retire, step back, or just stop. You need to know the number — the actual, honest, sustainable number — before you make the call.
